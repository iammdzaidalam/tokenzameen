TokenZameen — Purchase Marketplace 
Detailed Product Requirements Document (PRD) 
Product 
TokenZameen Purchase Platform 
Objective 
Build a premium, conversion-focused property discovery and purchase platform within the 
TokenZameen website where users can discover, compare, enquire about, reserve, and initiate the 
purchase of curated real-estate opportunities. 
The platform will organize properties into five primary categories: 
1. Apartments  
2. Villas  
3. Commercial Spaces  
4. Sustainable Living  
5. Plots  
The experience should combine: 
• Luxury real-estate discovery  
• Investment intelligence  
• Transparent property information  
• Interactive property exploration  
• Lead generation  
• Site-visit booking  
• Purchase/enquiry workflow  
• TokenZameen's advisory layer
